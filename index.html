FLAGSHIP RESEARCH

Automated policy generation for fragmented business processes

When a BPMN process is split into fragments that run independently, each fragment still needs a governance policy that captures who can do what and under which conditions, without losing the intent of the original process. This project, carried out during my research internship with CERIST and UDST Qatar and continued as my engineering thesis, addresses exactly that gap.

I designed a pipeline of five specialized agents coordinated through the FIPA-ACL communication protocol. A Graph Builder agent reconstructs the process structure, an Exception Handler manages irregular cases, a Semantic Validator checks the result against SHACL constraints over a JSON-LD and RDF representation, a Fragment Policy Generator produces the policy itself using GPT models served through Azure OpenAI, and a Syntax Audit Agent verifies the output. Together they implement a Business-to-Policy algorithm that outputs ODRL 2.2 policies covering both authorized and denied fragment behavior.

I evaluated the system across incremental configurations using a Syntax Fidelity Score, BERTScore and F1-score, alongside an LLM-as-Judge setup comparing Kimi-K2.5 against Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct.
